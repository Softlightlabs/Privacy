Ritual Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Ritual is a habit tracker by Softlight Labs. You can use it without creating a Ritual account. This policy describes the Android app and the services involved in using it.

Your habit data

Ritual stores your habit names, icons, completion dates, schedules, pause/archive history, display order and reminder settings in the app's private storage on your device. It also stores your theme choice, introduction status and local records used to avoid duplicate reminders. Statistics are calculated from your saved history.

Ritual has no account service, habit-sync service or backup server. Softlight Labs does not receive your habit content through those services. The app has no ads, product analytics or crash-reporting service. Google Play and Android may process information independently as described below.

Reminders

Reminders are optional. If you enable one, Ritual may ask Android for notification permission. A reminder notification includes the habit name. Ritual requests private lock-screen visibility and supplies a generic public version without the name, but Android, your settings and device integrations determine what is actually visible. People who can see your notifications may see your habit names. Android also controls notification history. Ritual tries to restore enabled reminders after a restart; Android may delay or block delivery.

Widget visibility

If you add the Ritual Plus Today widget, it can show daily progress and up to three unfinished habit names, depending on its size. Without confirmed Plus access, Ritual renders a neutral card. Anyone who can see the widget may see its contents. Your launcher or device may keep a previous view until it refreshes. The widget opens Ritual; it does not mark habits complete directly.

Export and import

Manual export and import are available without a subscription. An export is an unencrypted JSON file containing your habits, completion/schedule/lifecycle history, order and primary reminder time. Additional Plus reminder times, purchase access, theme choice and introduction status are not included.

You select the destination or source through Android's document picker. A cloud-backed provider may upload and retain the file under its own privacy terms. Ritual reads or writes the document you select; it does not run automatic cloud sync. Keep export files private and delete unwanted copies through the provider. Import shows a preview; replacing existing habit data requires a separate confirmation.

Google Play purchases

Ritual uses Google Play Billing for Ritual Plus purchases, prices and ownership checks. A configured app may check prices and ownership when you open or return to Ritual, even if you do not start a purchase. The purchase flow opens after your choice. Some testing builds may have purchases unavailable.

Google Play handles your Google account and payment information. Ritual does not receive your payment-card details. Ritual uses product and offer information, purchase status, signed purchase records and purchase tokens to check access and acknowledge purchases. These operations may use the network. Ritual does not send habit names, history or export files to Billing, and it has no app-owned persistent purchase-token or Plus-access cache.

Google Play and its Billing software may process transaction, device and operational information under Google's own practices. The absence of Ritual analytics does not mean Google Play or Android processes no data. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Restore purchases restores Google Play ownership only. It does not restore habit data. To recover habit data after loss or on another device, import a previously saved habit export. An export does not grant purchase ownership.

Retention and deletion

Ritual does not automatically expire old habit history. Deleting a habit removes its associated local history and reminder records. A confirmed replacement import replaces the local habit dataset. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-owned local data and preferences; without an export, Ritual cannot recover your habit history for you. These actions are not a promise of secure erasure from every device or system copy.

Ritual requests that Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer be disabled for its app data. Device and Android behavior can vary; do not rely on system transfer as a habit-recovery method.

Deleting a habit, clearing data or uninstalling does not delete exported files, provider copies, Android notification history or Google's purchase records. It also does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage subscriptions through Google Play, and manage external copies and records through their respective providers.
